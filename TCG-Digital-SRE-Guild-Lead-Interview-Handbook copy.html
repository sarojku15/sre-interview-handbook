TCG Digital, at a glance

TCG Digital is the AI and digital arm of The Chatterjee Group (TCG) — a multi-billion-dollar conglomerate spanning petrochemicals, life sciences, real estate, and IT. TCG Digital itself runs on a delivery model built around cross-functional pods organized under specialized "Factory" units (Cloud Factory, UX Factory, Mobility Factory, and — per this JD — a Dev Factory), blending onshore and offshore talent per engagement.

Their flagship platform, mcube, won the ESC National Export Excellence Award for Best AI Product in 2025. Recent public messaging leans heavily into agentic AI, including in regulated (GxP/pharma) environments — worth knowing since it signals the reliability bar the SRE guild will be held to. Company size is roughly 500–1,000 employees globally, HQ functions in New York with a large Kolkata (Salt Lake, Sector V) base. Mantra: "Velocity to Value."
