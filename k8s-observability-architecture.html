§10

Best Practices

  • Use HA for all critical components
  • Centralized logs with retention & archive
  • Define SLOs / SLIs & Error Budgets
  • Use labels & annotations for discovery
  • Use recording rules to reduce load
  • Secure Prometheus, Grafana, Elasticsearch
  • Monitor the monitoring (meta-monitoring)
  • Regular backup & disaster recovery test
  • Capacity planning & scaling
  • Log PII masking & compliance
